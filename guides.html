Licence Renewal Guides

How renewal actually works, state by state

All fees checked against the regulators on 13 November 2026.

Renewing a trade licence isn't just paying the number on a notice. The regulator, the term and the late-fee rules all change from state to state. These guides cover what electricians, plumbers and builders actually pay in NSW, VIC, QLD, WA and SA for 2026-27, and how to avoid paying more than you need to.

Five states side by side

Here's roughly what a sole trader pays per year of licence, using the cheapest standard renewal option for an individual. It's a rough guide only: terms differ, and your category may cost more.

Approximate cost per year to stay licensed as a sole trader
StateElectricianPlumberBuilder
NSW$238$238$474
VIC$118$508$332–$520 + renewal
QLD$561 per renewal$105$356

NSW: 3-year individual contractor licence. VIC electrician: licence (A) + REC over 5 years. VIC plumber: registration + licence averaged over 3 years. VIC builder: annual fee by category, plus the 5-yearly renewal fee. QLD electrician: work licence + contractor licence, term not stated in the fee table. QLD plumber: occupational licence only (5 yrs); a plumbing business also needs a QBCC contractor licence. QLD builder: SC2, 3 yrs, from QBCC's fee page dated 1 July 2025. WA electrician: electrician licence + electrical contractor's licence. WA plumber: contractor licence (3 yrs). WA builder: practitioner + individual contractor registration (3 yrs). SA electrician and plumber: PGE contractor licence + worker registration. SA builder: general building work licence + supervisor registration. Details and sources are in each guide.
