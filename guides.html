Licence Renewal Guides

How renewal actually works, state by state

All fees checked against the regulators on 13 November 2026.

Renewing a trade licence isn't just paying the number on a notice. The regulator, the term and the late-fee rules all change from state to state. These guides cover what electricians, plumbers and builders actually pay in NSW, VIC and QLD for 2026-27, and how to avoid paying more than you need to.

The three states side by side

Here's what a sole trader pays per year of licence, using the cheapest standard renewal option for an individual contractor. It's a rough guide only: terms differ, and your category may cost more.

Approximate cost per year to stay licensed as a contractor, 2026-27
TradeNSWVICQLD
Electrician (worker + contractor)$238$118$561 per renewal
Plumber$238$508$105 (occupational, 5 yrs)
Builder$474$332–$520 + renewal$356 (SC2, 3 yrs)

NSW: 3-year individual contractor licence. VIC electrician: licence (A) + REC over 5 years. VIC plumber: registration + licence averaged over 3 years. VIC builder: annual fee by category, plus the 5-yearly renewal fee. QLD electrician: work licence + contractor licence, term not stated in the fee table. QLD plumber: occupational licence only; a plumbing business also needs a QBCC contractor licence. QLD builder: QBCC fee page dated 1 July 2025. Details and sources are in each guide.
