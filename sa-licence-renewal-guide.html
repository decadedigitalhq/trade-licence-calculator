SA Licence Renewal Guide

Renewing your SA trade licence

South Australia puts plumbers, gas fitters and electricians on one licence type, renews contractor licences every year, and runs a separate 3-year cycle for workers. Here's what each trade pays.

Fees checked against SA.GOV.AU's renewal page (last updated 6 October 2026) on 13 November 2026.

Who you renew with

All building and trade licences in South Australia are issued by Consumer and Business Services (CBS). Unlike the eastern states, SA doesn't split electricians and plumbers across different regulators: plumbing, gas fitting and electrical work all come under the same plumbing, gas fitting and electrical (PGE) contractor licence and worker registration.

Two cycles to keep track of

  • Contractor licences renew every 12 months. That covers both PGE contractors and building work contractors.
  • Worker registration renews every 3 years. This applies to plumbing, gas fitting and electrical workers, the registration that lets you do the work yourself.

A sole trader usually holds both, so you'll pay something every year and something extra every third year, often on different dates.

Electricians, plumbers and gas fitters

PGE renewal fees (CBS)
Licence or registrationHow oftenRenewal fee
Individual contractorEvery year$504.00
Body corporate (company) contractorEvery year$737.00
Individual worker's registrationEvery 3 years$342.00

Worked example: a sole-trader sparky or plumber holding a contractor licence and a worker registration pays $504 × 3 + $342 = $1,854 over 3 years, or $618 a year. If you hold the registration but work for someone else and don't contract, it's just $114 a year.

Builders

General vs specified: "specified building work" is a single trade such as bricklaying or carpentry. "General building work" covers whole building projects. CBS says sole traders normally hold a contractor's licence and a building work supervisor registration, because both are needed to contract work. On the figures above, a sole-trader general builder with both pays $851 a year.

How to renew, step by step

  1. List what you hold: contractor licence (yearly) and worker or supervisor registration. Note each expiry date.
  2. Have your licence number and a credit card ready. That's what CBS asks for to renew online.
  3. Renew online through CBS before the expiry date.
  4. Update your details while you're there. CBS lets you update your address at renewal, and some changes must be reported within 14 days anyway.
  5. Download the digital licence in the mySAGOV app if you want it on your phone. Hard copies are still issued too.
