WA Licence Renewal Guide · 2026-27

Renewing your WA trade licence

What electricians, plumbers and builders pay to renew in Western Australia this financial year, and why picking a longer term won't save you money here.

Fees checked against the WA Government's Building and Energy fee schedule (2026-2027, from 1 July) on 13 November 2026.

Who you renew with

In WA, trade licensing for electricians, plumbers, gasfitters and builders is handled by Building and Energy, part of the WA Government. Each trade has its own board (the Electrical Licensing Board, the Plumbers Licensing Board and the Building Services Board), but the fees, renewals and payments all go through Building and Energy.

The WA quirk: longer terms don't save money

In NSW and Queensland, a longer licence term works out cheaper per year. In WA it doesn't. The 2026-27 multi-year fees are exact multiples of the 1-year fee: an electrician licence is $124 for 1 year or $620 for 5 years, which is still $124 a year.

So what's the point of the longer term? Fewer renewals to remember, and you lock in this year's price. WA fees are reviewed every year with changes usually taking effect on 1 July, so paying for 5 years now protects you from future increases.

A sole-trader electrician usually holds both the electrician licence and the electrical contractor's licence. That's $777 a year in 2026-27, one of the dearest combinations of the five states we cover.

If it lapses: restoring an expired licence costs the same as a new application, including the non-refundable application fee. A 1-year electrician licence costs $218 to restore instead of $124 to renew. A 1-year contractor's licence costs $823 instead of $653.

WA separates the practitioner registration (you, the qualified builder) from the contractor registration (the business). A sole trader who holds both pays $1,407 every 3 years, about $469 a year. A company contractor registration costs far more, at $3,880 for 3 years.

How to renew, step by step

  1. Check every licence or registration you hold and each expiry date. Electricians and builders often hold two, and they may not expire together.
  2. Pick a term where there's a choice. It costs the same per year in WA, so choose based on how settled your business is.
  3. Renew online before the expiry date through Building and Energy, using the link on your renewal notice.
  4. Lodge on time. Builders can be charged a $65 late lodgement fee, and an expired electrical licence has to be restored at the new-application price.
  5. Keep the receipt and updated licence details for head contractors who ask.
