Set up my reminder

How it works

  1. Pick your state, trade and expiry month. You'll find the expiry date on your licence card or your regulator's online account.
  2. Confirm your email. We send one email with a link. Nothing else is sent until you click it.
  3. Get two reminders: 3 months before expiry, then 1 month before. Each one has this year's fee, the best-value term and a link to the renewal guide for your state.
  4. We delete your details after your expiry month. Sign up again for your next renewal.

Why 3 months?

It's the window that matters in all three states. Victorian builders who lodge their renewal less than 3 months before expiry pay a $342.80 late fee. In NSW, if you're late, the restoration fee only applies for 3 months after expiry; after that you may have to apply again. And Energy Safe Victoria opens the online renewal 3 months before your expiry date.

What we keep

Only your email, state, trade and the month your licence expires. No name, no licence number. Your details are used for these reminders and nothing else, never sold, and deleted after your expiry month or as soon as you unsubscribe. See our privacy policy.
