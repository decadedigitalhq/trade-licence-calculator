About this tool

No guesswork on renewal costs.

Trade Licence Calculator AU is a free tool built for Australian electricians, plumbers, and builders who want a quick, straight answer on what their licence renewal actually costs — without digging through a state regulator's fee schedule PDF.

We only publish figures we can verify directly from official sources. Where a fee genuinely isn't published as a flat number — because it's calculated from a formula, or scales with your business turnover — we say so, and link you straight to the right official page instead of guessing.

What this site does

Select your state, trade, and renewal length to see either an exact fee (where published) or a clear estimate range with a direct link to your regulator's own fee calculator.

What this site doesn't do

This isn't legal or financial advice, and it doesn't replace your official renewal notice. Fees change annually and can include discounts, surcharges, or category-specific variations we can't account for automatically.

Our commitment to accuracy

Every figure on this site is sourced from a state regulator (NSW Fair Trading/Building Commission NSW, QBCC, or the Victorian Building and Plumbing Commission) at the time of writing, with the source linked at the bottom of the calculator page. We review these periodically, but regulators update fees annually — always confirm against your actual renewal notice.

Spotted an outdated figure? Let us know.
