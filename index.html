NSW renewal fees, 2026-27

NSW contractor licences are issued by Building Commission NSW. Electrical, plumbing, draining and gasfitting are "specialist work", so electricians and plumbers renew a trade or specialist contractor licence. Builders renew a building contractor licence, which costs about twice as much.

Individual contractor licence renewal, July 2026 – June 2027
Licence1 year3 years5 years
Trade or specialist (electrician, plumber)$299$715$1,231
Building contractor$592$1,422$2,475
Qualified supervisor or tradesperson certificate$33$66$113

Worth knowing: the 3-year term is the cheapest per year in NSW, not the 5-year. A trade licence works out at $238 a year over 3 years and $246 a year over 5. For builders it's $474 a year over 3 years against $495 over 5.

VIC renewal fees, 2026-27

Victoria has two regulators. Electricians renew with Energy Safe Victoria. Plumbers and builders renew with the Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC). Both now publish dollar amounts for each year, so you don't need to work anything out from fee units.

Frequently asked questions

Is a longer licence term always cheaper per year?

No, and NSW is the clearest example. For 2026-27 a trade or specialist contractor licence costs $299 for 1 year, $715 for 3 years ($238 a year) and $1,231 for 5 years ($246 a year). So the 3-year term is the best value. The 5-year term still saves you two renewal rounds and the risk of forgetting one.

What does it cost to renew late?

Each state handles it differently. In NSW, if you lodge within 3 months after expiry you pay a restoration fee instead of the renewal fee: $367 rather than $299 for a 1-year trade licence. After that you may need to apply again from scratch. In VIC, builders pay a $342.80 late fee if they lodge less than 3 months before expiry, so you can pay a late fee before your registration has even expired. In QLD, QBCC lists a $142.70 restoration fee on top of the renewal fee.

Do these fees include GST?

The BPC says all its fees are GST-exempt. Government licence fees in NSW and QLD are also generally charged without GST. Your renewal notice shows the final figure.

When do the fees go up?

On 1 July each year. For 2026-27 Queensland applied a 3.4% indexation rate. Victoria's fee unit rose from $16.81 to $17.27. A figure from last year's renewal notice is almost certainly out of date.

Do I need insurance to hold a trade licence?

Sometimes it's a licence condition. Victorian building practitioners must give the BPC proof of the required insurance every year, or their registration is suspended. NSW builders doing residential work over the threshold need Home Building Compensation cover. Even where it isn't a condition, most builders and principal contractors ask for a public liability Certificate of Currency before you start.
